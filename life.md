# Life’s Purpose: Knowing God Through Jesus Christ

Life raises questions that success, wealth, and praise cannot fully answer. Why are we here? What gives our days meaning? Where can we find lasting love? Life is about knowing God, and God makes himself known through Jesus Christ.

This gives life a personal purpose. We are not here only to reach goals or gather experiences, though these can be valuable. We are invited to know and love the One who made us. Jesus says in John 17:3, “Now this is eternal life: that they know you, the only true God, and Jesus Christ, whom you have sent.” Eternal life is more than living forever. It is life rooted in knowing and loving God.

To know Jesus is to see what God is like. In John 14:9, Jesus says, “Anyone who has seen me has seen the Father.” Jesus cares for those who suffer, welcomes those who are rejected, and shows mercy to those who sin. His actions reveal a God who cares about human pain. His love for truth and justice also shows that God’s love does not mean approving of everything we do. It calls us away from what harms us and leads us toward what is good.

The cross shows this love most clearly. Jesus’ death reveals both the depth of God’s love and our need to be brought back to him. Jesus’ rising from the dead gives us hope that sin and death do not have the final word. Knowing God through Jesus means more than admiring a wise teacher. It means trusting the Savior who gave himself for the world.

Knowing Jesus changes how we live. His words call us to trust him and put his teaching into practice. We deepen our relationship with him as we speak to him in prayer, listen to him through Scripture, worship him, and help one another follow his ways. This relationship takes shape in daily choices: forgiving when it is easier to hold a grudge, serving without seeking praise, and loving people whom others ignore.

This purpose does not take away our daily duties. It gives them new meaning. Work becomes a chance to serve faithfully. Family life becomes a place to practice patience and kindness. Even in suffering, when life is hard to understand, we can learn to depend on God. Knowing Jesus does not promise a life without trouble, but it assures us that we do not have to face trouble alone.

Knowing God through Jesus is both our life’s purpose and a daily journey. It invites us to move beyond talking about God to sharing our lives with him. The deepest purpose of life is not to achieve more, but to know Jesus more fully and, through him, to know the heart of God.
