# Power = Word of God × Faith

Great power can work through little faith. The picture of God’s Word multiplied by faith points to a simple Christian truth: God can do great things through even a little faith. This is not because our faith has endless strength, but because the God we trust has endless power.

If everything depended on the size of our faith, we could easily lose heart. Our trust sometimes weakens, we do not understand everything, and we may feel afraid. Yet Jesus taught that faith as small as a mustard seed could move a mountain (Matthew 17:20). His words turn our eyes from our weakness to the greatness of God. Even a small seed of true faith matters when we place it in him.

This picture becomes clearer when we consider who the Word of God is. John writes, “In the beginning was the Word, and the Word was with God, and the Word was God” (John 1:1). He then says, “And the Word was made flesh, and dwelt among us” (John 1:14). This living Word is Jesus Christ.

Faith in the Word, then, is more than believing a sentence or an idea. It is trusting the living Christ. The Bible shows us who he is and teaches us to trust his goodness, his promises, and his authority. The power does not come from us. It belongs to the Lord we trust.

Still, this picture is not a formula for controlling God. Faith does not make him grant every wish. Instead, faith trusts his promises, follows his lead, and rests in his wisdom. Moving mountains is his work, not the result of how hard we think or how strongly we feel.

We do not need great faith before we begin trusting Jesus. We can bring him the little faith we have and follow his word. What matters most is not how much faith we have, but whom we trust.

Little faith can face great mountains because Jesus, the living Word, is greater than them all.
