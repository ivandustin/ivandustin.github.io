# Value = People × Service × Quality

Value comes from helping people well. The more people you help, the more useful your service is, and the better you provide it, the more value you create.

Service means meeting people’s real needs. It is not just about offering something. It means understanding what people need and helping them in a useful way.

Quality means how well you serve people. It includes care, skill, and the ability to solve a problem, improve a situation, or make someone’s life better.

Because people, service, and quality work together, each one affects the total value. You may offer only one kind of service, but if you do it very well, it can create great value.
